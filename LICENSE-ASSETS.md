## What counts as artwork · 哪些算素材

- the twenty channel graphics — 20 个频道图形（`ui/js/cel.js`, `ui/js/graphics.js`）
- the sixty stickers — 60 个贴纸（`ui/js/stickers.js`）
- the twelve colour block and edge shapes — 12 种色块与分界线形状（`ui/js/render.js`）

This covers the designs themselves and any image rendered from them. The source code that draws
them remains under the AGPL like the rest of the program.

范围是这些图形的设计本身，以及由它们渲染出来的图像。绘制它们的源代码与程序其余部分一样，仍适用 AGPL。

## Attribution · 署名

When you redistribute or adapt the artwork, credit it, for example:

再分发或改编这些素材时请署名，例如：

## Exception for covers · 封面的例外

As an additional permission from the copyright holder: **covers you make with SmedCover need no
attribution**, even when they contain the built-in artwork. You may publish and use them, commercially
or not, without crediting SmedCover. Attribution is required only when the artwork is redistributed
on its own — as an asset pack, for instance — or used in another product.

作为版权人给予的附加许可：**用 SmedCover 制作的封面无需署名**，即使其中包含内置素材。你可以发布和使用
这些封面，包括商用，不必注明 SmedCover。只有把素材单独拿出来再分发（例如做成素材包），或用于其他产品时，
才需要署名。
