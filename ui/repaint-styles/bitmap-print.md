Rebuild the attached photograph as a fine bitmap editorial print — a large dot-matrix image on paper, calibrated toward fine bitmap print posters, not chunky pixel art.

1. Subject: one main element, or two coordinated ones at most, taken from the photo — a person, animal, flower, food, plant, object or a clear silhouette of the scene — large enough to carry the picture (about 40–80% of it) with deliberate negative space. For a person, a close-up face or head-and-shoulders crop is stronger than a full figure; keep the face refined and attractive but visibly bitmap-translated — eyes, nose, lips, hair and cheek shadows made of fine halftone dots and small square pixels, never smooth photographic detail.
2. Bitmap construction, one dominant method: a fine visible square-pixel grid, small dot-matrix halftone, stepped aliased edges, low-resolution scan lines, missing-pixel gaps, broken grid marks or density-shifted dot fields. Detail is suggested by pixel density, dot spacing and absence; tones change gradually from denser to looser marks, never a solid flat fill (except a small anchor area). Pixels are visible at normal size but never oversized or blocky.
3. Colour: one dominant ink with subtle density variation — soft cyan-blue, magenta-pink, dusty rose, violet, lime or pale green, black or soft grey — chosen from the photo's mood. A second colour only as a visibly secondary registration offset, thin bitmap curve, ghost layer or soft wash. No rainbow palettes.
4. Ground: scanned paper or lightly tinted print stock — warm off-white or faint ivory for warm subjects, pale grey or pale blue-grey for cool, portrait or night subjects — with subtle fibres, faint toner dust and low-opacity registration specks; quiet, never dead digital white, never dirty. Alternatively a full pale coloured paper ground with soft blurred ink fields, from which the subject emerges in reverse halftone gaps.
5. Print process across the whole image: dot gain, risograph-like ink, printer noise, slight misregistration, broken scanline gaps, uneven halftone density; flat, no 3D depth. Mood: quiet, delicate, observational, early-digital, poetic.

Avoid: retro video-game pixel art, sprites, 8-bit icons, Minecraft or voxel looks, game or app interfaces, kawaii or chibi characters, cartoon stickers, neon or vaporwave glow, glossy 3D, cinematic lighting, depth of field, airbrushed illustration, smooth stippling, watercolour, scrapbook collage, loud pop art.
