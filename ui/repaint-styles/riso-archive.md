Rebuild the attached photograph as one quiet risograph archive print: the photo's observable facts reprinted as a sparse cluster of printed marks.

1. Deconstruct the photo: the primary element and up to two secondary relationships (identity, shape, count, position, scale, direction); its structure (one dominant axis, rhythm of intervals and clusters, depth order, occlusion, a meaningful empty field); its large-area colour temperature; and two or three dominant colour roles.
2. Every mark traces to one of those facts; delete decorative marks. Use simplified printed-matter silhouettes, blocks, hairlines, dots and short bars — identity readable, detail dropped — with one primary motif family and at most two supporting ones. Preserve counts, uneven spacing, size hierarchy, direction, asymmetry and occlusion; reflections and shadows become fainter echoes. Sample repeated supporting elements sparsely (three to five representatives, never a full row). People become small anonymous silhouettes, animals featureless silhouettes, never faces.
3. Never trace the whole scene: no sky region or ground plane running edge to edge. One visual cluster of 10–25% of the picture, off centre where the photo's structure suggests, with 65–85% empty paper; no edge-hugging, no grid alignment.
4. Paper follows the photo's temperature: a cool photo (blue or grey sky, snow, water, green fields) on pale blue-grey porcelain or cool snow-white paper; a warm one (dusk, autumn, earth, indoor light) on warm pale cream. Full-frame, flat, matte, with subtle fibres and faint mottling — light, never deep yellow or stained.
5. A two- or three-colour riso print: each colour role becomes one ink layer (deep indigo, leaf green, vermilion-orange…), printed as grainy silhouettes and fields with photocopy softness, fine riso grain, ink bleed at edges and slight misregistration where layers overlap. Inks stay saturated; the most vivid one covers the primary element or a substantial field, not a tiny dot. Grey or black hairlines may support.
6. Mood: quiet, archival, diary-like, nostalgic but chromatic.

Avoid: a full-bleed scene or landscape painting, cartoon or picture-book flatness, a commercial poster layout, clean UI white, cinematic light, 3D, neon, photorealism, muddy browns.
